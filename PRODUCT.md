# Product

## Register

product

## Users

IT service agents and ticket managers at hotel groups, plus operations and hotel leaders who need clear oversight across one or more properties. They use the app during shifts to manage incoming IT tickets, assign work, and monitor issue resolution without wasting time in navigation.

## Product Purpose

A hotel group IT helpdesk system for reporting incidents, managing priority work queues, and tracking operational performance across hotels. Success is a reliable, low-friction workflow that keeps ticket status visible, reduces triage time, and helps teams respond faster to overdue and high-risk incidents.

## Brand Personality

Clear, reliable, efficient

A practical and reassuring tool with a calm professional tone that emphasizes predictability, focus, and operational confidence.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered or flashy dashboard visuals, oversized gradient marketing heroes, and consumer-style SaaS landing page tropes. Do not drift toward dark terminal-style or gamified interfaces; keep the UI grounded in real work and operational clarity.

## Design Principles

- Make work visible first: prioritize ticket status, assignments, and risk indicators over decorative content.
- Keep controls predictable: use direct labels, stable structure, and minimal surprise in ticket actions.
- Support time-critical workflows: let users reach overdue, due-soon, and unassigned work quickly.
- Clarify hotel scope: make it obvious when views are filtered to a single hotel versus group-level data.
- Build calm confidence: favor restrained color, measured hierarchy, and consistent spacing to reduce cognitive load.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Use clear language, consistent control labels, and reduced-motion-friendly transitions. Support Thai/English toggling and make key workflows easy for users with varying language and familiarity levels.
